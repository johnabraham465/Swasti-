Offers & multi-buy deals

Set prices like 1 for $2.99 · 2 for $5 · 4 for $9. They come off automatically as items are scanned or tapped, always at the best price for the customer. An offer can cover a whole brand or category, so different flavors and sizes count together.

Bulk import / update inventory

Upload a CSV file (save from Excel or Google Sheets as CSV) or paste rows below. Columns: name, category, variant, price, stock, sku, barcode (or UPC), brand. A row whose barcode or SKU already exists updates that item. Rows with the same name but different variants become one item with variants.

Rewards program

Customers earn points on every purchase. When they reach a reward level, they can use it as money off a later sale, and those points are deducted. Add as many levels as you like.

Age-restricted sales

Set a minimum age for categories such as tobacco, vapes or alcohol. When a sale includes them, the register asks for ID before payment. Scan the barcode on the back of any US driver license or state ID with a 2D scanner or the phone camera, and the sale is approved or blocked automatically. Expired IDs are refused.

Export a scan data file

One row per item sold or returned, with UPC, quantity, price, store and manufacturer discounts, multi-pack, loyalty and age-check flags. Dates default to last week (Sunday–Saturday).

Card payments

Card payments go straight from your processor to your bank. SWASTI never holds your money. Your MID prints on card receipts.

Barcode scanner

USB and Bluetooth scanners work as soon as they’re plugged in or paired: there’s nothing to install. The POS recognizes a scan by its speed, whether the scanner ends with Enter, Tab or nothing. UPC-A, EAN-13, UPC-E, EAN-8, GTIN-14, and codes without the check digit all match the same item.

Roles & permissions

Turn functions on or off for Managers and Cashiers. Owners can always do everything. Pages that are switched off disappear from the menu; blocked actions ask for approval. Changes save right away.
